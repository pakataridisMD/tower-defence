# The Unbound: a bestiary of the six hordes

Generated by `tools/campaign/horde.py` (edit the table there, not this file). Each land is invaded by the horde from across the ring, led by that horde's Herald. Every kind of the horde was shaped by the art of its kingdom, and every one has a power of its own.

## The Tide (Tidelore), invading Frostmere

Led by Saltmother Ourra. Element: Water.

### Brinecaller

*Risen. A drowned tide-priest. Its brine puts out burns and washes poison and frost off the horde around it.*

Tidefolk priests are drowned under the ice to be initiated and brought back up by their own people. These were brought back by the Saltmother instead. They came up still praying, salt in their mouths, ladling the cold sea over anything that burns.

Health 70, speed 66, armour 0, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `douse`. Affinity: attuned.

### Drowned Netcaster

*Risen. A drowned fisherman who casts a weighted net over the strongest tower near him.*

The fleets of Pearlhaven went down in the first winter of the Opposites War. Their crews never stopped fishing. Now they cast for whatever stands on the shore, and a tower caught in their nets swings slow and heavy as a drowned man's arm.

Health 80, speed 64, armour 1, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `net`.

### Mire Eel

*Wild. A drowned eel as long as a boat. Every few seconds it dives under the road, out of reach, and swims faster.*

Tidelore remembers everything water has touched, and the deep remembers its eels. They slide up the flooded roads in the dark of the Tide's rain, a lantern lure swinging over a mouth of needles, and vanish under the mud the moment anything looks at them.

Health 46, speed 92, armour 0, bounty 4, lives 1. Power: `submerge`.

### Fogwife

*Hollowed. A faceless drowned seer. She breathes sea-fog over the towers near her, and they cannot see as far.*

The great seers of the Tide Throne paid for every vision with a memory. The Fogwives paid with all of them, their names, their faces, their children. What is left breathes the fog of a sea that has forgotten where the shore is.

Health 55, speed 70, armour 0, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `fog`. Affinity: warded.

### Rimeback

*Wild. A crab the size of a cart, crusted in old ice. Its rime shell soaks up blows until it breaks, then grows back.*

Dragged up from under Frostmere's ice by the mad Sea-Queen's voice, the Rimebacks carry a winter on their backs. Break the crust and the cold grows it back over them, layer on layer, the way the lake freezes a little more each night.

Health 120, speed 50, armour 3, bounty 7, lives 2. Power: `rime_shell`. Affinity: tank.

## The Stone (Runecarving), invading Sunscar Canyon

Led by Gorrath the Quarried. Element: Earth.

### Oathbroken

*Hollowed. A Stoneborn turning to stone for a broken oath. Every blow it takes grows more stone over it.*

A rune can never be uncarved, and a Stoneborn who breaks a carved oath turns slowly to stone. The Mountain-King's madness broke every oath in Kharum at once. The Oathbroken march with granite growing through their skin, and hurting them only hurries it.

Health 110, speed 58, armour 1, bounty 6, lives 1. Power: `accrete`.

### Quarry Hound

*Wild. A hunting hound carved from quarry stone. It charges down the road now and then, too heavy to slow or push.*

Runewrights of the Deep Halls carved hounds to run down oath-breakers, and an oath of pursuit gave them legs. Orrok's madness pointed them at the Steppe. When one catches the scent it charges, and nothing short of a mountain slows it.

Health 70, speed 82, armour 1, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `charge`. Affinity: attuned.

### Runebearer

*Hollowed. Carries a carved oath-stone on its back. Every ally near it gains 5 armour.*

Oath-keepers carry the great vows of Kharum carved in standing stones, so that whole companies can swear by them. The runes still burn. Every Stoneborn within sight of one stands as if the mountain stood behind it.

Health 140, speed 50, armour 3, bounty 8, lives 2. Power: `bulwark`. Affinity: warded.

### Geode Brute

*Wild. A boulder split open on a heart of crystal. Splash, chains and bursts lose most of their bite on its facets.*

The deepest halls of Kharum hold stones that were never cut, only listened to. Orrok's voice woke them. They stand up split open on hearts of violet crystal, and any blow spread thin across them is scattered like light through a prism.

Health 160, speed 50, armour 2, bounty 9, lives 2. Power: `facets`.

### Menhir Walker

*Wild. A walking standing stone. The horde near it cannot be slowed or pushed back.*

The standing stones of the Thunder Steppe were set by the Galeborn to hold the sky still for a moment of prayer. Someone carved a king's face into one. Now it walks, and where it stands the ground holds fast: frost, tide and thunder cannot move what walks beside it.

Health 200, speed 42, armour 4, bounty 10, lives 2. Power: `steadfast`. Affinity: tank.

## The Hollow (Starcraft), invading Hollow Rift

Led by the Pale Vesper. Element: Void.

*Hollowed. A Veilwalker who spent all her light on starcraft. Her black lantern dims the towers near her.*

Every spell of starcraft darkens something of the caster: hair, eyes, shadow. Lightdrinkers kept casting after the light ran out, and now they borrow it. Her lantern burns black, and towers in its gloom strike like men fighting in the dark.

Health 65, speed 68, armour 0, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `eclipse`. Affinity: attuned.

### Umbral Stalker

*Wild. A great cat made of the dark between stars. Towers only see it when it comes within half their range.*

The Star-Queen's hunters rode cats of living night on the road of the dead. Without their riders they hunt alone, pelts full of tiny stars. You do not see one coming. You see it arrive.

Health 45, speed 108, armour 0, bounty 4, lives 1. Power: `stalk`.

### Gravecaller

*Hollowed. A deathwalker who walks the road of the dead the wrong way. He calls the horde's fallen back up.*

Veilwalkers walk the dead to the edge of the dark and leave them there. The Gravecallers walk back carrying them. Where the horde falls near one, the dead get up again, slower, quieter, still marching.

Health 80, speed 60, armour 0, bounty 6, lives 1. Power: `raise`.

### Wailing Widow

*Risen. A widow who followed her husband down the road of the dead. Her dying scream silences the towers near her.*

She would not let him walk the road alone. Nyxara's madness opened it, and she went down it after him, and found it empty. She has been screaming ever since. Struck down, the scream breaks loose, and nothing near her can work its art until it fades.

Health 60, speed 70, armour 0, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `wail`. Affinity: warded.

### Voidmaw

*Wild. A thing that is nearly all mouth. It heals whenever the horde dies near it.*

Something grew in the dark at the bottom of the Rift while the Veilwalkers mapped the stars above it. It has a crown of eyes and a mouth full of other stars, and it follows the horde for what falls.

Health 130, speed 55, armour 2, bounty 8, lives 2. Power: `devour`. Affinity: tank.

## The Flame (Kindling), invading Ember Reach

Led by Ashwick the Kindled. Element: Fire.

### Cinderhound

*Wild. A hound of living cinder. Burns heal it instead of hurting it, and frost melts off it twice as fast.*

The Forge-King's hunting dogs lay down in his forges when he slept and got up when he went mad. Their ribs glow through the cinder of their hides. Feed them fire and they only run harder.

Health 50, speed 104, armour 0, bounty 4, lives 1. Power: `fireborn`. Affinity: attuned.

### Smoke Wraith

*Wild. The smoke of a burned town that would not blow away. About one blow in three goes straight through it.*

When the first towns of the Ember Reach burned, their smoke did not rise. It walked. There is an ember at the heart of each one, the last warm hearth of a house no one lives in, and most of what is thrown at it passes through soot.

Health 50, speed 86, armour 0, bounty 5, lives 1. Power: `smoke`.

### Glass-Singer

*Hollowed. A cold old Kindler who sings glass into being. She wards three allies with glass that stops a tower's blow.*

Kindlers pay with their own heat. The Glass-Singers sang sand into glass until they had none left: their arms are blue, their rune tattoos still glowing. Their song hangs panes of ringing glass before the horde, and each one stops a single blow before it shatters.

Health 65, speed 66, armour 0, bounty 6, lives 1. Power: `glass_ward`. Affinity: warded.

### Slagwalker

*Hollowed. A smith-lord sealed in plates of slag. Heavily armoured, but every hit knocks some of the slag away.*

The smith-lords of Pyrrhenhall turned anger into blades. When the Forge-King went mad, some turned themselves. They walk in fused plates of slag with the furnace still white inside, and every blow that lands knocks loose a plate that never grows back.

Health 120, speed 50, armour 12, bounty 8, lives 2. Power: `meltdown`.

### Brass Juggernaut

*Wild. A walking siege furnace. It gets faster the longer it walks unchecked; a slow, stun or push resets it.*

Siege engines of the Ember Throne, fed on coal and kindled runes, built to batter down the gates of Pearlhaven. The Forge-King's madness woke the fires in their bellies. They start slow. Left alone, they do not stay slow.

Health 220, speed 40, armour 5, bounty 12, lives 3. Power: `momentum`. Affinity: tank.
