## The Last Lantern (`prologue`)

*Before the first stage. The opening story video, about 2 minutes.* About 2:49.

**1. [An old painted map coming out of darkness: six kingdoms in a ring, each under its own crown and colour (red, blue-white, green, teal, ochre, violet), and a seventh, colourless, in the middle.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Two thousand years ago there were seven kingdoms: six in a ring, each with its own magic, and Orrun in the middle, where all their roads met.

**2. [The High King's armies crossing a burning land; behind them the flames freeze into grey glass, the grass stops moving, a river hangs still in mid-fall.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The songs say Orrun's king, Vael-Orrun, wanted all six crowns. He learned a power that makes magic stop. Where his armies walked, fire would not burn.

**3. [A chasm splitting the eastern land, its walls dropping into darkness. Far below, a stone door as tall as a mountain swinging shut on a grey army. The six monarchs on the rim, pushing.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The six monarchs beat him. They drove him and his army down the Rift, a chasm miles deep, and shut them behind the Black Gate at the bottom.

**4. [The crown shattering into a six-coloured crystal, the Heartstone. The six monarchs fading into a volcano, a storm, a tree, a sea, a mountain, a starless sky.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** They made its key from his crown: the Heartstone. Then they gave their lives to keep the Gate shut. Their spirits sleep in their lands. We call them the Wardens.

**5. [Dawn over Solenne: six white walls rising on black ruins, the Heartstone glowing at the centre, the golden Sunstone above.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** On his ruins we built Solenne, my city, with the Heartstone at its heart and the Sunstone above it, a lens whose light the things of the dark cannot bear.  
*(pause)*  
That is how the songs tell it.

**6. [Night. Cassivane Dral, the Lord Marshal, alone on a balcony, holding an open gold locket with a girl's portrait. His face is in shadow.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Six years ago, my Lord Marshal, Cassivane, lost his daughter Ilyra in the Rift.

**7. [The bottom of the Rift. The Black Gate grinding open; grey shapes pressing out of the dark behind it.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** This spring, somebody unlocked the Black Gate. We did not know who.

**8. [Oswin, old, grey robes and hat, at the foot of the Gate with his lantern-staff raised. Behind him a line of Spirewright towers burning in six colours, their beams on the door.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Oswin, the master of our Order, turned the light of all his towers on it and drove it back, until the gap was only as wide as a cart.

**9. [The ground under Oswin gives way. He falls through the gap into the dark. Last frame: the small white light of his lantern, still burning, falling away.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Then the ground gave way, and he fell through.  
*(pause)*  
His lantern was still lit.

**10. [A single-file column of the Unbound marching out of the gap and up the Rift road, splitting into six. At the head of each column, a grey-eyed Herald in the dress of a different people.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Six armies came out through the gap, each led by a grey-eyed champion we called a Herald. Each Herald went home, to the land they were born in. A Warden lets its own people come close.

**11. [Six quick cuts: fire walking out of a volcano onto a forge-town, lightning striking a tent camp on a plateau, walking oaks and thorns over rope bridges, drowned climbing out of holes in sea ice, red canyon walls breaking into walking stone, darkness climbing out of the Rift over violet grass.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** There each Herald whispered to its Warden until the Warden went mad. A mad Warden turns its own element on its own people. The volcano poured out its fire. The forest strangled its towns. And the Herald raised an army out of the land itself.

**12. [Queen Ysolde on the white walls of Solenne, looking east toward smoke on the horizon. Title card.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** I am Ysolde, Queen of Solenne. You are a Spirewright, the last of Oswin's apprentices. You build the towers.  
*(pause)*  
We take the lands back one at a time. Hold the road to each town. Kill its Herald. Free its Warden.  
*(pause)*  
Hold the road.
