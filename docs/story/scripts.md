## The Last Lantern (`prologue`)

*Before the first stage. The opening story video, 1 to 2 minutes.* About 2:33.

**1. [An old painted map coming out of darkness: six kingdoms in a ring, each under its own crown and colour (red, blue-white, green, teal, ochre, violet), and a seventh, colourless, in the middle.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Before the Concord, before the Spire, before anyone kept count of the years... there were seven kingdoms.

**2. [Six crowned monarchs, each working their art: a forge-king hammering glowing runes, a horse-king singing lightning down, a green queen with a sprouting seed, a sea-queen reading ice, a mountain-king carving a rune into rock, a star-queen tracing a star-chart in the air. Then the middle kingdom's black-walled capital, and on its throne a king in black with a tall crown of six empty sockets.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Each had its own magic. Forge-runes and storm-songs. Root-craft and tide-memory. Stone-oaths and star-charts. The seventh kingdom studied them all.  
*(pause)*  
And its king wanted all of it.

**3. [The High King's armies crossing a burning land; behind them the flames freeze into grey glass, the grass stops moving, a river hangs still in mid-fall.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** His name was Vael-Orrun, the High King. He learned to unmake magic. Where his armies marched, fire would not burn, and the wind lay down and died.

**4. [The six monarchs in a ring around the burning black city, hands joined, Fire across from Water, Storm across from Earth, Nature across from Void. The High King is flung out through the edge of a starfield into darkness.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The six hated one another. They stood together anyway, each beside its oldest enemy, and drove him out past the edge of the stars.  
*(pause)*  
We call it the Ring.

**5. [The crown shattering into a six-coloured crystal, the Heartstone. The six monarchs fading into a volcano, a storm, a tree, a sea, a mountain, a starless sky. Then dawn over Solenne: six white walls rising on black ruins, the Heartstone glowing at the centre, the golden Sunstone above.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** They could not kill him. So they locked the door with his own crown, and gave their lives to hold it. On the ruins of his city, we built Solenne.  
*(pause)*  
My city. That is how the songs tell it.

**6. [Night. Cassivane Dral, the Lord Marshal, alone on a balcony, holding a gold locket. Over his shoulder, in the stars, the faint crown-shaped gap. His face is in shadow.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Six years ago, a man I loved like a father lost his daughter to the dark. The exiled king made him a promise.

**7. [The Hollow Rift: violet grass, a ring of black pillars singing with stolen light. A black comet crosses the sky. The ground at the centre tears open into a crack full of nothing.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** On the night of the Black Comet, the edge of the world split open.

**8. [Oswin, old, grey robes and hat, walks to the lip of the Crack with his lantern-staff raised. Behind him a line of Spirewright towers burning in six colours. Grey shapes crawling out of the Crack.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Oswin, the Grey Lantern, held it for one night. At dawn, he walked to the edge... and gave it every light he had.

**9. [A blast of white light; the Crack slams almost shut. The ground under Oswin gives way. He falls into the dark, the lantern going out. Last frame: one tiny spark still drifting down after him.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The Crack closed to a hairline.  
*(pause)*  
And Oswin fell. His lantern went out.

**10. [Six quick cuts: a volcano burning grey, a stormcloud gone solid black, a giant tree rotting, a frozen sea heaving, a mountain opening grey eyes, a starless sky with a crown. Then hordes of grey-eyed elemental spirits pouring across the land toward each other.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** But a voice can pass through a hairline. It drove the six sleeping monarchs mad, and set each land against the one across from it.  
*(pause)*  
The High King has come back for what was his.

**11. [A long line of refugees with carts on an ash road. Tamsin Reed with her lantern-pole waving them on. A gloved hand sets a glowing keystone into the foundation of a stone tower.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** We cannot win back the lands. We can only hold the roads until their people escape.  
*(pause)*  
Every road we hold will cost us a city.

**12. [Queen Ysolde on the white walls of Solenne, looking east toward smoke on the horizon. Title card.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** I am Ysolde, Queen of a city that is running out of cities. And you, Spirewright, are the last of Oswin's apprentices.  
*(pause)*  
Hold the road.

## The Night of Falling Stars (`night_of_falling_stars`)

*After 12 stages won. The midpoint disaster, about 2 minutes.* About 1:52.

**1. [Solenne at dusk, the Sunstone glowing over a city packed with refugees. Lanterns everywhere. Quiet.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** We had lost twelve cities by then. Solenne was still bright. We thought that meant something.

**2. [The great east gate swinging open. Soldiers in Lanternguard blue standing aside... their eyes empty and grey. Behind the gate, the Unbound pour in.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** At midnight, the Dawn Gate opened from the inside.

**3. [Hollowed soldiers fighting in the streets; refugees running; Lanternguard fighting their own.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** They were soldiers we had fed and armed. He had been keeping them in our own barracks, under our own colours.

**4. [Old Fennick in the gatehouse, spectacles on, firing half-built towers that crackle with unstable elements. Brannoc forging the outer bars shut with his hammer if he is with us.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Fennick held the gate. He had a handful of half-built towers, and a hammer, and a temper.

**5. [Fennick shouting over his shoulder, then turning back to the gate as it starts to fall.]**

> **MASTER TOBIAS FENNICK:** Spirewright! Fire burns, water slows... and you place them where I tell you.  
*(pause)*  
Go. Get her out.

**6. [The gatehouse collapsing in a cloud of dust and coloured sparks. Silence.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The gatehouse came down on him. He did not let go of the gate until it did.

**7. [Cassivane at the top of the Spire, his red armour starting to blacken, holding a crystal blade glowing six colours. The Sunstone lens huge above him.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Above it all, on the Spire of Aurel, the Lord Marshal raised a blade made from a piece of our Heartstone...

**8. [The Sunstone shattering. Its pieces fall across the city like burning stars.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** ...and he broke the sun.

**9. [Ysolde in the palace courtyard, looking up as the light falls on her face; white-out. Tamsin dragging her into cover.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** The light fell on me as I ran. It was the last thing I ever saw.

**10. [Cassivane, ash drifting from his armour, stepping through a rip of darkness at the top of the Spire, carrying the Sunstone's shards in a net of grey light.]**

> **CASSIVANE DRAL:** There is a world without death, Ysolde. I am going to bring her home. I am going to bring all of them home.

**11. [Over the dark city, one falling shard of white light drifts sideways, east, toward the distant Rift, and keeps going.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** Not all of the shards went with him. One of them fell east, and did not land. Nobody saw it but me... and I did not know what I had seen.

**12. [Dawn hour. A black sky over Solenne. Only torches. Refugees looking up.]**

> **NARRATOR (YSOLDE):** When the sun should have risen, it did not.  
*(pause)*
